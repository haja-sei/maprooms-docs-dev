## About Maprooms

Maprooms is a web application for exploring climate information through
interactive maps and charts. It supports historical climate analysis,
monitoring, forecasts, and future projections, with products for agriculture,
water, health, and disaster risk management.

Developed within the ENACTS initiative, Maprooms helps National Meteorological
and Hydrological Services and Regional Climate Centres make climate
information accessible to sector specialists and other users.

## About this guide

This documentation supports developers who install, configure, extend,
and maintain Maprooms. It explains how Flask, Python calculation scripts,
YAML configuration, Jinja templates, and JavaScript work together.

Follow the guide to prepare your environment, configure datasets, create
and register modules, and connect their maps and charts.

Start with the [overview](1-overview.md) for background, explore the
[documentation outline](2-outline.md), or select a chapter from the
navigation sidebar.
